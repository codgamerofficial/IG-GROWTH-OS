# GlowFit AI — 60 to 90-Second Hackathon Demo Script

> **Target Duration:** 75–90 Seconds  
> **Speaker Role:** Product & AI Integration Lead  
> **Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges (Perfect Corp. / Beauty Tech)

---

### [0:00 – 0:10] The Core Problem
*(Screen: GlowFit AI Landing Page with cinematic hero and scanning visual)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Beauty shopping online has a massive confidence problem. Customers don't know what suits them, which shade won't turn ashy, or whether a ₹1,500 cart will actually look good in person. Returns exceed 30%.  
> Meet **GlowFit AI** — an AI beauty shopping decision engine powered by YouCam."

---

### [0:10 – 0:25] Selfie & Optical Skin Analysis
*(Action: Click "Analyze My Skin", select one of the high-res sample portraits or snap webcam, watch the scanning animation reveal the Beauty Profile)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "We begin with a single portrait. Using YouCam's computer vision surface optics, GlowFit extracts objective cosmetic characteristics: skin tone, undertone harmony, surface texture, and natural radiance.  
> Notice that we never make medical diagnoses — we calibrate cosmetic formulations directly against optical measurements."

---

### [0:25 – 0:40] Beauty Intent & Smart INR Budget
*(Action: Navigate to Beauty Intent. Select "Wedding Guest", choose "Soft Glow" style, select the "₹1,500" budget preset)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Next, the user selects their goal: 'Wedding Guest', style: 'Soft Glow', and sets a smart budget in Indian Rupees — say, ₹1,500.  
> Our recommendation engine immediately constructs a complete signature look: Base, Lips, Cheeks, and Eyes. Every single product has a transparent 'Why This Product' explanation showing why it suits their undertone and budget."

---

### [0:40 – 0:60] The Key Experience: YouCam Virtual Try-On
*(Action: Click "Virtually Try Look". Move the interactive drag comparison slider back and forth. Zoom in on the lips and cheek glow. Switch to split side-by-side mode)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "Here is the core magic: YouCam Look Virtual Try-On.  
> Customers can interactively drag our comparison slider to see their natural baseline against the complete finished look. They can switch templates in real time, inspect pigmentation depth, and zoom in with complete clarity.  
> The customer sees the look on themselves *before* spending a single rupee."

---

### [0:60 – 0:75] The Smart Beauty Cart & Commerce Loop
*(Action: Click "Add Look Products to Cart", review the budget tracker, tap "Proceed to Demo Checkout" to trigger the confetti celebration and order reservation)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "With one click, the entire look translates into a Smart Beauty Cart. Our budget meter verifies that the ₹1,446 selection fits perfectly within the ₹1,500 limit.  
> The customer can save the look to their archive or check out immediately."

---

### [0:75 – 0:90] Impact & Closing
*(Screen: Return to the Saved Looks gallery showcasing previous looks)*

> **Speaker:**  
> "By connecting YouCam's skin analysis, neural virtual try-on, and smart budget economics, GlowFit turns beauty shopping from guesswork into confidence.  
> Thank you."
