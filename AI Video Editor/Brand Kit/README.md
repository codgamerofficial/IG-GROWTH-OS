# AI Video Editor — Brand Kit

## Purpose
This directory holds the definitive visual and typography rules for your brand. It is dynamically refined whenever you analyze reference videos or update your design preferences.

## Files
- `brand-kit.md`: Human and LLM-readable design rules (Hex codes, typography, caption styling, camera movement, cut pacing).
- `brand-tokens.json`: Machine-readable tokens used by the Remotion and HyperFrames rendering engines.
