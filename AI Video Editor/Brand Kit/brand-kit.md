# Brand Kit — GlowFit & RIIQX AI Video Intelligence

## 1. Color Palette (Exact Hex Codes)
- **Primary Obsidian Background**: `#0C0C0E` (Deep cinematic black)
- **Surface Elevation Dark**: `#18181B` (Rich dark slate for cards & panels)
- **Primary Electric Gold**: `#FFD43B` / `#F59E0B` (High-retention highlight, CTA, key word emphasis)
- **Cyber Cyan Highlight**: `#06B6D4` / `#00F2FE` (Technology metrics, price drops, futuristic accents)
- **Emerald Profit Green**: `#10B981` (Savings, discounts, arbitrage profit margin)
- **Crisp High-Contrast Text**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white headers & primary caption body)
- **Subtle Muted Meta**: `#94A3B8` (Timestamp, secondary descriptions, borders)

## 2. Typography & Font Matching
- **Primary Display / Headlines**: `Montserrat` / `Cabinet Grotesk` (Weight: 800 ExtraBold, uppercase tracking +0.05em)
  - *Fallback if system font*: `Arial Black` / `system-ui`
- **Body & Subtitles**: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weight: 600 SemiBold)
- **Monospace Numbers & Prices**: `JetBrains Mono` / `SF Mono` (Weight: 700 Bold for ₹ INR & $ USD prices)

## 3. Caption Style & Animation
- **Format**: Dynamic word-by-word karaoke pop with 2 to 4 words visible at once.
- **Position**: Center-lower third (`y: 72%` from top of frame, safe from TikTok/Reels UI overlay).
- **Background**: Translucent rounded pill (`rgba(12, 12, 14, 0.75)`, padding: 8px 16px, border-radius: 12px).
- **Active Word Color**: Glow yellow `#FFD43B` with 1.15x scale spring bounce.
- **Inactive Word Color**: Crisp white `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Shadow**: `0px 4px 14px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## 4. Camera Movement & Framing
- **Framing**: Clean vertical rule-of-thirds; subject eye line at upper horizontal third.
- **Digital Push-In**: 1.00x -> 1.08x scale linear or subtle ease-in-out on core arguments (2.5 second duration).
- **Punch Cut (Jump In)**: 1.15x scale crop on punchline words or dramatic reveal moments.
- **Motion Blur**: 180-degree shutter simulation on whip transitions.

## 5. Cut Frequency & Pacing
- **Hook (0.0s - 1.8s)**: Fast visual cut or motion within first 1.2 seconds.
- **Average Cut Interval**: Every 1.8 to 2.4 seconds.
- **B-Roll Layering**: Maximum 3.0 seconds continuous A-roll before inserting B-roll overlay, product 3D rotate, or screen capture.
- **Audio Hit Sync**: Visual cuts align with snare hits or bass drops within a ±35ms tolerance window.
