# AI Video Editor — Approved Videos

## Purpose
This directory archives all finalized, client-approved, or creator-verified video edits.

## Contents & Organization
- High-resolution final renders (`1080x1920` vertical 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/TikTok or `1920x1080` horizontal 16:9 for YouTube).
- Naming convention: `[PROJECT_ID]_[TITLE]_APPROVED_v[VERSION].mp4`
- Metadata sidecar JSON files preserving final render parameters, audio loudness levels (LUFS), and export timestamps.

## Feedback Loop
Videos moved here serve as ground truth for training the AI Video Editor on what you consider "perfect" editing quality.
